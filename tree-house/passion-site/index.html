BUGS CAN TEACH US ABOUT LIFE buglife.org
    Internal Growth: Just as a pupa changes invisibly inside a cocoon, human milestones require quiet, internal periods of hard work before outward success shows.
    Invisible Foundations: Insects like bees, worms, and ants hold up entire food webs, proving that minor or unseen efforts support the survival of everyone else.
    Evolutionary Survival: Insects outlived the dinosaurs and adapt rapidly to pesticides, symbolizing human grit, survival instincts, and the ability to thrive through catastrophic change.
